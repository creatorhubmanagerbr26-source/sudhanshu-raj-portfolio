About Me

I am a Class 12 PCB student at Hill Spring International College, Kalyan, with a strong interest in teaching and education technology. Alongside my studies, I have gained teaching experience in Mathematics and Science for students from Classes 4 to 7, across CBSE and Maharashtra State Board.

I am passionate about making learning simple, practical, and accessible for students. My long-term goal is to build a career in the EdTech industry and use technology to make quality education more affordable and accessible to students from different backgrounds.

I believe that education should not be limited by financial barriers, and I aspire to contribute to a learning environment where every student gets an opportunity to learn, understand, and grow.
